**EcogeoAgent**
EcogeoAgent is a multi-agent system I am developing to bridge AI and economic geography. It ingests user-uploaded spatial data and autonomously uncovers spatial relationships, interprets spatial evolution, and generates forecasts. The system comprises multiple specialised agents: a data-reading agent, a spatial-relationship analysis agent, a spatial modelling agent, and a spatial prediction agent. By orchestrating these agents, EcogeoAgent aims to offer a scalable, interpretable tool for researchers and policymakers to understand regional dynamics and anticipate future spatial patterns.

**01. Global Patent Database**
Coverage  
Global patent records, with more than 120 million observations collected from Google Patents.
Unit of Observation  
Patent / patent publication.
Key Variables  
Patent identifiers; dates; applicants and inventors; technology classifications; titles and abstracts; priority information; citation information; and other bibliographic metadata.

**02. China Patent Transfer Database**
Coverage  
Comprehensive patent transfer records in China from 1986 to 2025.
Unit of Observation  
Patent transfer transaction.
Key Variables  
Patent information; transfer date; transferor; transferee; transferor address; transferee address; geographic information; and associated patent characteristics.

**03. China Trademark Database**
Coverage  
Comprehensive trademark registration records in China from 2000 to 2025.
Unit of Observation  
Trademark application / registration.
Key Variables  
Trademark identifiers; applicants / registrants; applicant addresses; trademark status; goods and services categories; and related registration information.

**04. Hong Kong Trademark Database**
Coverage  
Hong Kong trademark registrations from 2010 to 2025, based on records from the Hong Kong Intellectual Property Department.
Unit of Observation  
Trademark application / registration.
Key Variables  
Trademark identifiers; application and registration dates; registrants; registrant addresses; trademark classifications; goods and services categories; and geographic information.

**Data Access and Research Collaboration**
These databases were independently collected, processed, standardised, and structured for academic research. They can be used individually or combined with other spatial, economic, demographic, patent, firm, and urban datasets to investigate a wide range of questions related to innovation, technology transfer, knowledge flows, entrepreneurship, and regional development.

If you are interested in using any of these datasets, please feel free to contact me to discuss data access and potential research collaborations. I am also happy to discuss potential research questions and empirical projects that can be developed using these data.
