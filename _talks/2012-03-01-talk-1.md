This talk provides an overview of the historical development, emerging opportunities, key challenges, and major planning insights for the high-tech industry in the Pearl River Delta. Drawing on years of practical experience in regional planning, the presentation examines how the PRD has evolved into a global manufacturing and innovation hub, the policy and institutional factors that have shaped its industrial trajectory, and the spatial and economic disparities that pose challenges to its future development. Key planning insights emphasize the importance of fostering innovation ecosystems, promoting industrial upgrading, and addressing regional inequalities to sustain long-term growth.
