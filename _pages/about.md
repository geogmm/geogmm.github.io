I am currently a PhD candidate at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. My research interests lie at the intersection of economic geography, innovation studies, and urban networks. My current work centres on the uneven regional costs and benefits arising from technological change.

Prior to my doctoral studies, I spent five years as a senior urban planning engineer in China’s public sector, working on high tech industry planning and land use planning in the Pearl River Delta. This experience gave me first hand insight into how cities and regions pursue industrial upgrading, cultivate emerging industries, and maintain sustained economic growth. It also motivated me to pursue academic research, with the aim of translating rigorous analysis into practical guidance for regional policy making.

In my spare time, I am building a multi agent AI system that explores regional economic evolution through spatial, temporal, and relational data. I plan to make the system openly available on Hugging Face. Through this project, I aim to build an open, reproducible platform that combines open data, computational modelling, and AI, making it freely available to the research community to foster collaborative inquiry into urban and regional change.
